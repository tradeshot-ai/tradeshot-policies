Getting started

How does TradeShot work?

Take a photo of your customer's existing space (kitchen, bathroom, garden, exterior — anywhere you'll be doing work), describe the job in a sentence or two, and TradeShot's AI generates a realistic visualisation of the finished result in about 30 seconds.

You can save the result, share it with your customer, edit the prompt to fine-tune, or generate again from scratch.

What kind of jobs does it work for?

TradeShot is designed for renovation and improvement work where the visual result matters to the customer — kitchens, bathrooms, extensions, landscaping, decorating, flooring, exterior work. It's less useful for invisible work (plumbing repairs, electrical rewiring, etc.) although you can still use it for project management.

Which devices are supported?

TradeShot is currently iOS only and requires iOS 15 or later. An Android version is on the roadmap.

How do I get the best AI results?
  • Use a well-lit photo with the whole space visible
  • Avoid photos with people or moving objects in the frame
  • Describe what you want in plain English — materials, colours, finishes
  • Be specific: "oak shaker units, white quartz worktop, herringbone floor" beats "modern kitchen"
  • If the first result isn't quite right, use the edit prompt to tweak — don't start over

AI generation

Why is my generation taking so long?

Most generations take 20-40 seconds. If yours is taking longer, it's usually one of:

  • Slow internet connection — try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data
  • Large photo file size — try retaking with a smaller photo
  • High demand on the AI service — wait a minute and try again

If a generation fails, you won't be charged against your monthly quota.

The AI got it wrong — what can I do?

AI generation isn't perfect. You have two options:

  • Edit prompt — describe just the small change you want ("make the worktop darker", "change the flooring to oak"). The AI will keep everything else the same and only adjust what you mention.
  • Generate again — start over with a fresh prompt. Each generation produces different results.

Don't expect pixel-perfect accuracy — these are visualisations to help discussion, not technical drawings.

Can I use the generated images commercially?

Yes. You own the AI-generated images produced from your photos and prompts, and you can use them in quotes, customer presentations, on your website, and on social media. See our Terms of Use for full details.

We do recommend including a brief disclaimer alongside any image you share with customers — something like "AI visualisation for illustration only. Final result may vary."

Can I remove the TradeShot watermark?

The "Visualised with TradeShot AI" watermark appears on saved and shared images and is part of the Service's branding. It's not currently possible to remove it. If you have feedback on this, email us — we're listening.

Subscription & billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Find TradeShot and tap Cancel Subscription

You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

All refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the TradeShot purchase, and tap "Request a Refund". Apple usually responds within 48 hours.

We can't process refunds directly — Apple is the merchant of record for all subscriptions.

How does the free trial work?

New users get a 7-day free trial of TradeShot. You won't be charged until the trial ends. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial period ends through your Apple ID Subscriptions settings.

I was charged but my subscription isn't active

Try the following:

  • Open the app and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen
  • Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe
  • Make sure you're signed into the same TradeShot account you used at the time of purchase
  • Check the App Store for any pending updates

If it's still not working, email us with your Apple ID receipt or order number and we'll look into it.

Account & data

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to the Profile screen (top-right), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, photos, jobs, quotes, and visualisations.

Note: deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. Cancel via Apple ID settings first if you no longer want to be billed.

How do I change my business profile or logo?

Tap the Profile button at the top of the home screen. You can update your trading name, contact number, VAT number, and business logo. Tap Save Profile when done.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Photos are stored securely on Supabase infrastructure. We never sell your data to anyone. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and we'll send you a reset link. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

Troubleshooting

The app crashes or won't load
  • Force-close the app and reopen it (swipe up and flick the app away)
  • Check the App Store for an available update
  • Restart your device
  • If it persists, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version
Photos in the gallery are slow to load

The first time you open a gallery item, the AI-generated images load from cloud storage. After that, they cache locally and open instantly. If loading takes longer than 10 seconds, check your internet connection.

I can't take or upload a photo

Make sure TradeShot has permission to access your camera and photo library:

  • Open Settings → TradeShot
  • Make sure Camera and Photos are both enabled
The wrong business name shows in the app

Sign out and sign back in. If you've previously used TradeShot with a different account on the same device, some cached data may need clearing — signing out resets it.
